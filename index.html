01 What is CAPS?

CAPS is a small, honest proof-of-work coin with three jobs:

🍾

A themed collectible

A fun, wasteland-flavoured bottle-cap currency with its own art and lore. Something to earn, hold, and trade for the love of it.

🛠️

Learn the whole stack

Mine it with an ASIC or a CPU, run a full node, browse the block explorer, and hold your own keys in a non-custodial wallet. No gatekeeping — the entire pipeline is open.

🤝

A community currency

Used for tips, prizes, and perks across the undergroundroots community. Value comes from being used, not from hype.

Transparency note: block 1 contains a 470,000 CAPS premine (used to seed the pool and community). Every other block pays the standard 50 CAPS reward. SHA256d proof-of-work, LWMA difficulty — the same battle-tested machinery as Bitcoin, just tuned for a community-scale coin.

03 Get started

⛏️ Mine CAPS

Point any SHA-256 miner at the community pool and get paid to your own CAPS address.

# ASIC miners
stratum+tcp://asicpool.space:8433   user: <your caps1 address>   pass: x

# CPU / low-difficulty
stratum+tcp://asicpool.space:8434   user: <your caps1 address>   pass: x

Don't have an address yet? Create one in the web wallet — your keys stay in your browser.

🖧 Run a full node

Build capsd from source (or grab a binary below), then drop this in your caps.conf:

server=1
daemon=1
txindex=1
rpcuser=choose_a_user
rpcpassword=choose_a_strong_password
addnode=caps.undergroundroots.ca

Start it with capsd and check progress with caps-cli getblockchaininfo. Default P2P port is 12566.

🔎 Explore & 🔐 self-custody

Watch the chain on the block explorer, and hold your own CAPS in the non-custodial web wallet (keys are generated, encrypted, and used entirely in your browser).
