01 What is BTLCAP?

BTLCAP is a small, honest proof-of-work coin with three jobs:

🍾

A themed collectible

A fun, wasteland-flavoured bottle-cap currency with its own art and lore. Something to earn, hold, and trade for the love of it.

🛠️

Learn the whole stack

Mine it with an ASIC or a CPU, run a full node, browse the block explorer, and hold your own keys in a non-custodial wallet. No gatekeeping — the entire pipeline is open.

🤝

A community currency

Used for tips, prizes, and perks across the undergroundroots community. Value comes from being used, not from hype.

Name note: the coin is being renamed from CAPS → BTLCAP to avoid a clash with an unrelated project. It's a rebrand, not a relaunch — the same chain, the same blocks, and every coin you already hold carry straight over. Only the name, ticker, logo and address spelling change.

04 Get started

⛏️ Mine BTLCAP

Point any SHA-256 miner at the community pool and get paid to your own address.

# ASIC miners
stratum+tcp://asicpool.space:8433   user: <your address>   pass: x

# CPU / low-difficulty
stratum+tcp://asicpool.space:8434   user: <your address>   pass: x

Today the network uses caps1… addresses; they become btlcap1… after the cutover (see roadmap). Don't have one yet? Create it in the web wallet — your keys stay in your browser.

🖧 Run a full node

Build capsd from source (or grab a binary below), then drop this in your caps.conf:

server=1
daemon=1
txindex=1
rpcuser=choose_a_user
rpcpassword=choose_a_strong_password
addnode=caps.undergroundroots.ca

Start it with capsd and check progress with caps-cli getblockchaininfo. Default P2P port is 12566. (On-disk tool names stay caps* through the rebrand.)

🔎 Explore & 🔐 self-custody

Watch the chain on the block explorer, and hold your own BTLCAP in the non-custodial web wallet (keys are generated, encrypted, and used entirely in your browser).
